What this is. A time-optimal router for the BYC Big Windward–Leeward Regatta (course 1 to Point Bonita, course 2 to EASOM, signalled at the 11:00 warning), solving an earliest-arrival Dijkstra over a tidal current field built from 8 NOAA current stations plus hand zones for the water NOAA does not instrument — including the Cityfront flood back-eddy and the Yellow Bluff ebb back-eddy, which are what decide this race.

What to distrust. The Olson 25 polar is constructed, not measured: absolute times are only as good as that table, though lane comparisons are far more robust because the error largely cancels. The coastline is hand-digitized. The wind field has no shifts, gusts or puff structure, so the router never decides under uncertainty — it also knows the future exactly, which is worth real time on a course where the tide turns mid-race. Treat its elapsed time as an optimistic bound and its lane calls as the useful part. The SI 10 obstruction lines are hard barriers in the router; five of the six are taken from the NOAA ENC, the "H" beam is placed from the SI text only. Marks are routed to, not rounded — leave them to port yourself (SI 12.3).
